ABOUT THE ARTIST

Learning, creating, and building a style of my own.

I'm Devansh, a young artist exploring Lippan art, mandala art, relief work and Indian-inspired decorative design.

I enjoy the patience behind repeated patterns, the character of handmade textures and the way traditional inspiration can be expressed in a contemporary way.

This website is the beginning of my portfolio—a place to document my work, experiment with new ideas and grow as an artist.
