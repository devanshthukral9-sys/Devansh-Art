ABOUT DEVANSH

An artist learning to turn patience into visual language.

I’m Devansh, an emerging artist exploring Lippan art, mandalas, relief work and Indian-inspired decorative design.

I’m drawn to repetition, symmetry, texture and the patience required to make something by hand. My work is an ongoing experiment: taking inspiration from Indian artistic traditions and finding my own contemporary way to express them.
